What the account shows:

1. **Cost per order rose 3.5x, from CAD 39 to CAD 138.** It rose every month from Nov 2025. There is no single step that lines up with one platform change; it is a steady slide with two sharper drops (March 2026 in click-to-order rate, September 2026 in cost per thousand impressions).
2. **Clicks got 2.5x more expensive.** Click-through fell from 5.95% to 3.47% and cost per thousand impressions rose from CAD 64 to CAD 94.
3. **Fewer clicks buy.** Click-to-order rate fell from 2.76% to about 1.8 to 2.0%.
4. **Order value never moved.** It has sat at CAD 51 to 54 for 16 months.
5. **Meta has reported less revenue than spend since March 2026.**

**The cap does not fit the order value.** A USD 52 cap is roughly CAD 72 (assuming about 0.72 USD per CAD; confirm the rate). The first order is worth CAD 53. Even at the cap, each new customer costs more than their first order brings in, before product and shipping cost. The cap only works if repeat orders make up the difference. ## Plays

Ranked by evidence and fit with Meta-only, USD 60k a month, USD 52 cap.

### 1. Raise first-order value with a bundle ladder
- **Evidence:** order value flat at CAD 53 for 16 months (Observed, own data); competitor leads every ad with a multi-bottle saving (Observed).
- **Why first:** no ad improvement gets cost per order from CAD 138 to under CAD 53. The cap only becomes workable when the first order is worth more.
- **Skill:** `offers`, then `pricing`. **Effort:** a week. **Metric:** average order value; share of orders with 3+ bottles.
- **Prompt:** "Use the offers skill. Nutrively sells Soursop Green Drops at about CAD 53 a first order on Meta. Design a 1/3/6-bottle ladder with honest per-bottle savings, free-shipping threshold and a money-back guarantee, targeting a first-order value above CAD 110. Inputs I will give you: unit cost, shipping cost, current price."

### 2. Rebuild creative volume
- **Evidence:** click-through fell from 5.95% to 3.47% (Observed, own data); competitor ships dozens of new ads several times a day (Observed).
- **Skill:** `ad-creative`. **Loop:** weekly batch via `marketing-loops`. **Effort:** a week to first batch. **Metric:** outbound click-through, cost per click.
- **Prompt:** "Use the ad-creative skill. Write 30 ads for Soursop Green Drops across five angles (occasional bloating, daily energy, 10-second routine, four ingredients in one, money-back guarantee), three hooks each, two formats each. Structure/function claims only, with the FDA disclaimer. Fixed headlines: 'Save Up to [X]% on Soursop Green Drops Today' and 'Try It Risk-Free for [N] Days'."

### 3. Put the guarantee in every ad
- **Evidence:** 86% of competitor ads (Observed). **Skill:** `ad-creative`, `copywriting`. **Effort:** a day. **Metric:** click-to-order rate.

### 4. Fix the page before buying more clicks
- **Evidence:** click-to-order rate fell from 2.76% to about 1.9% (Observed, own data). Trustpilot complaints about subscription charges and cancellation (search summary, unverified).
- **Skill:** `cro`. **Effort:** a week. **Metric:** click-to-order rate back above 2.5%.

### 5. Move to the brand page, with real creators as partners
- **Evidence:** both advertisers run from brand pages; Nutrively runs only from persona pages (Observed). **Skill:** `influencer-marketing`. **Effort:** two weeks.

### 6. Launch Black Friday now
- **Evidence:** competitor live since Nov 6 (Observed). **Skill:** `launch`, `ad-creative`. **Effort:** days. Depends on play 1.

## Budget guidance

Do not raise spend to USD 60k a month yet. At CAD 138 an order, that budget buys roughly 600 orders worth about CAD 32,000 in first-order revenue (arithmetic from own data, assuming 0.72 USD per CAD). Hold spend near the current CAD 17,000 to 18,000 a month, run plays 1 to 4, and step spend up only when cost per order in testing is under the cap for a full week.

## Plays to skip

- **Parasite, detox and "flush" claims.** Competitors run them (Observed). They invite FTC, FDA and Meta enforcement on a supplement.
- **Matching 1,617 ads.** That volume follows from a working offer; it is not the cause of one.
- **Search content.** Domain Rating 0 and you said Meta is the only channel you can run.
- **Blaming platform updates.** The account shows a 12-month slide, not a step. The causes visible in the data are fixable.
