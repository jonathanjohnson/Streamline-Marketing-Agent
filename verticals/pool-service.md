# Vertical Playbook: Pool Service

Read with the active client's `product-marketing.md`. Client facts override anything here.

## Economics that drive every decision
- **The asset is the route, not the job.** Weekly service accounts are recurring revenue; the value of a new account is `monthly rate x expected tenure (often multi-year)`, plus repair and equipment upsell from the tech who sees the pad every week.
- **Route density beats volume.** A new account 2 minutes off an existing route is worth far more than one 25 minutes away. Geo-target paid media and LSA to ZIPs where routes are already dense; exclude or bid down outliers.
- **Three revenue lines, three different funnels:**
  1. Weekly service (recurring, low ticket, high LTV): LSA, GBP, referrals, neighborhood saturation.
  2. Repair and equipment (pumps, heaters, filters, automation; high ticket, often from existing customers): upsell flows, search for "pool pump replacement", "pool heater repair".
  3. One-offs (green-to-clean, acid wash, openings/closings, leak detection): seasonal search spikes, flat-rate offers, a feeder into weekly service.
- Ask for: price per month by pool type, churn rate, techs and stops per tech per day, repair/equipment gross margin.

## Seasonality
- **Sunbelt (AZ, FL, TX, NV, SoCal):** year-round service with a spring surge (Mar-May) when owners reopen or get serious, and summer peak demand for repairs and green pools. Winter is for retention, equipment upgrades (variable-speed pumps, automation) and heaters in cooler markets.
- **Seasonal markets:** openings (Apr-Jun) and closings (Sep-Oct) are the volume spikes; pre-book openings in Feb-Mar with early-bird offers, pre-book closings in Aug. Winter is dead for service, but fine for remodel and equipment lead gen.
- Build content and ad calendars a month ahead of each spike, not during it.

## Keyword clusters (validate volumes in Ahrefs/Semrush per market)
- **Service:** pool cleaning service, pool service near me, weekly pool service, pool maintenance [city], pool cleaning cost
- **Problem / urgent:** green pool cleanup, cloudy pool water, pool algae removal, pool pump not working, pool heater not heating, pool leak detection
- **Seasonal:** pool opening service, pool closing service, winterize pool
- **Equipment:** variable speed pool pump, pool pump replacement cost, pool heater installation, salt system conversion, pool automation
- **Remodel adjacent:** pool resurfacing, acid wash pool, pool tile cleaning
- Programmatic SEO fit: `[service] in [city/neighborhood]` pages only where real route coverage exists; thin city pages for areas they don't serve hurt more than they help.

## Channel mix
- **Google LSA (pool cleaning category):** usually the highest-intent, lowest-friction source for weekly service. Dispute bad leads every week; respond to messages fast because the ranking rewards responsiveness.
- **GBP:** primary category usually "Pool cleaning service"; add secondary categories that match real services (pool repair, pool supply store only if they have retail). Post weekly with before/after green-to-clean photos (these perform). Run geo-grids around route clusters via GMB Crush.
- **Google Search:** repair and equipment terms, green pool, openings/closings. Separate campaigns per revenue line so bidding isn't polluted by low-value service clicks.
- **Meta:** neighborhood-radius campaigns around existing routes, before/after creative, "your neighbors on [street] use us" social proof. Lead forms with a qualifying question (pool type, current service) cut junk.
- **Referral and saturation:** door hangers on neighbors of new accounts, Nextdoor, realtor and home inspector partnerships (new homeowners with pools).
- **Route acquisition:** buying routes from retiring operators is a real growth lever. Prospecting skill can build target lists.

## Offers that work
- First month (or first cleaning) free / discounted with a minimum term
- Flat-rate green-to-clean with conversion to weekly service at a discount
- Free equipment inspection with first service
- Early-bird opening/closing pricing with prepay
- Variable-speed pump upgrade framed on energy savings (cite utility rebate programs only after verifying they exist in that market)

## CRO notes
- Price transparency ("weekly service from $X/mo") lifts form conversion in this category; hiding price pushes people to call three competitors.
- Above the fold: service area, "same tech every week", licensed/insured, reviews count, a short form (name, phone, ZIP, pool type).
- Track phone calls as conversions with a duration threshold; most bookings are calls.

## Retention
- Service reports after every visit (photo of the pool, chemical readings) are the strongest churn reducer and a source of review requests.
- Annual price increase messaging, equipment health reminders, winter upgrade campaigns.

## Compliance and trust
- State/local contractor licensing varies (some states license pool contractors or require it for equipment/electrical work). Confirm before writing "licensed" claims.
- CPO (Certified Pool Operator) certification is a strong trust signal when techs hold it.
- Chemical safety claims: don't promise "chemical-free"; salt systems still produce chlorine.

## KPIs
Cost per booked weekly account, account churn per month, accounts per route, repair revenue per account, LSA lead dispute rate, review velocity.

## Upstream skills to lean on
`ads`, `seo-audit` (local SEO references), `programmatic-seo`, `offers`, `referrals`, `churn-prevention`, `prospecting` (route acquisition, realtor partners), `social`, `emails`, `sms`.
