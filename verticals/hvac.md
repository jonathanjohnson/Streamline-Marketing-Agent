# Vertical Playbook: HVAC

Read with the active client's `product-marketing.md`. Client facts override anything here.

## Economics that drive every decision
- **Three funnels, wildly different values:**
  1. Repair / emergency (urgent, weather-driven, $ hundreds): the volume engine and the entry point to replacements.
  2. Replacement / install (high ticket, often with financing): where the profit is. Many replacements originate from a repair call where the tech recommends replacing.
  3. Maintenance memberships (recurring, low ticket): smooth seasonality, keep the customer, feed the replacement pipeline.
- Measure channels on **revenue per lead** and **replacement opportunities created**, not cost per lead. A $40 tune-up lead that becomes a $14k system is the best lead in the account.
- Ask for: average repair ticket, average install ticket, repair-to-replacement conversion, membership count and renewal rate, call answer rate, booking rate by CSR.

## Seasonality and weather
- Cooling peak: first heat wave through late summer. Heating peak: first cold snap through winter. Shoulder seasons (spring, fall) are for tune-ups and membership sales.
- **Weather drives demand by the day.** Raise budgets and bids ahead of forecast heat waves or cold snaps; cut during mild stretches when capacity sits idle. Equally, cut spend when the board is full; leads you can't run in 24-48 hours mostly go to competitors.
- Pre-season tune-up campaigns (spring AC, fall furnace) build membership and surface replacements before peak.

## Keyword clusters
- **Emergency/repair:** ac repair near me, ac not cooling, furnace repair, heater not working, emergency hvac, heat pump repair, ac blowing warm air
- **Replacement:** ac replacement cost, new hvac system cost, heat pump installation, furnace replacement, ductless mini split installation, hvac financing
- **Maintenance:** ac tune up, furnace tune up, hvac maintenance plan
- **IAQ / adjacent:** indoor air quality, duct cleaning, air purifier installation, dehumidifier (upsell funnels)
- **Research content:** SEER2 ratings explained, heat pump vs furnace in [climate], how long do ac units last, R-410A phase-out and what it means for repairs (the refrigerant transition to A2L refrigerants such as R-454B and R-32 is a strong replacement-education topic).

## Channel mix
- **Google LSA (HVAC category):** usually a top performer for repair. Answer every call live; LSA ranking and booking rates collapse when calls go to voicemail.
- **Google Search:** split campaigns by repair, replacement and maintenance with separate budgets. Use call-only or call-heavy assets for emergency terms. Import offline conversions (sold jobs, revenue) from the FSM/CRM so bidding optimizes toward replacements, not cheap tune-ups.
- **GBP:** primary category "HVAC contractor" (or "Air conditioning contractor" / "Heating contractor" depending on the business mix). Geo-grids around the service area; photos of real installs; review responses mentioning the service and city.
- **Meta:** membership and tune-up promos, replacement financing offers, retargeting site visitors who viewed replacement pages. Seasonal urgency creative ahead of heat waves.
- **Email/SMS to the customer base:** the cheapest replacement leads come from aging-equipment campaigns to past customers (system age is in the FSM).
- **Manufacturer co-op:** brand dealer programs (e.g., dealer tiers) often fund part of the ad spend and require brand guideline compliance. Ask whether the client has co-op dollars.

## Offers that work
- Tune-up special that requires booking a membership or rolls into one
- Membership: priority service, no overtime fees, discount on repairs, two tune-ups a year
- Replacement: financing ("as low as $X/mo"), free in-home estimate, extended labor warranty
- Repair-credit-toward-replacement (apply the diagnostic/repair cost to a new system)
- Utility rebate stacking where programs exist (verify in the client's market; federal 25C credits ended for equipment placed in service after 2025, so don't lead with tax credits)

## CRO notes
- Separate landing pages per funnel; emergency pages lead with phone number, availability ("open now", "same-day") and service area; replacement pages lead with financing, estimate booking and comparison content.
- Online booking (ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro scheduling) lifts conversion for maintenance and non-urgent repair.

## Compliance
- **Financing ads:** stating a monthly payment, APR, down payment or term triggers Truth in Lending (Regulation Z) disclosures. Use the lender's approved language and include required terms, or keep it general ("financing available, subject to credit approval").
- State contractor licensing numbers in ads where required.
- Efficiency/savings claims ("save 40% on energy bills") need substantiation; prefer ranges tied to SEER2 ratings and "up to" qualifiers.
- Manufacturer trademark and co-op ad guidelines.

## KPIs
Revenue per lead by channel, replacement opportunities created, booked-call rate, call answer rate, memberships sold and retained, cost per sold install, average ticket by source.

## Upstream skills to lean on
`ads`, `conversion-tracking` (offline conversion import is the biggest win in most HVAC accounts), `attribution`, `seo-audit`, `content-strategy`, `offers`, `emails`, `sms`, `churn-prevention` (memberships), `cro`.
